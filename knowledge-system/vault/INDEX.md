## Status vocabulary

- **CONFIRMED**: direct user's requirements.
- **ACCEPTED**: explicit architectural policy adopted for planning; not proof of implementation.
- **PROVISIONAL**: plausible approach awaiting evidence or tests.
- **OPEN**: unresolved question.
- **DEFERRED**: intentionally postponed implementation.
- **HISTORICAL**: preserved old work that may contain obsolete assumptions.
- **VERIFIED-TEST**: claim backed by a named, reproduced test result with environment and command.

Always avoid converting prototype simulator tests into claims about Android audio or actual visual quality.
