# JUAN AT PEDRO: MGA KUWENTO NG BAYAN NG MALIGAYA

**Format:** Original Filipino family adventure / mystery comedy, continuous 8-episode season. **Episode length goal:** approximately 24 minutes of audible dialogue and narration per episode. **Season 1 central mystery:** Sino ang naghahangad gawing pribadong pag-aari ang tubig ng Maligaya, at bakit may tinatanggal na mga pangalan sa matatandang mapa?

> **Adaptation status:** This is a newly authored shared universe **inspired by the many separate Juan / Pedro folktale traditions**, not a rediscovered canonical narrative. No original folkloric sources establish Maligaya, the characters' relationships here, Don Esteban, or the eight-episode arc.

## Main dramatic premise

Juan is known for finding the easiest route through life, sometimes through laziness and sometimes by noticing what hard workers miss. Pedro values honest work and protecting his neighbors but risks acting before checking evidence. Their friendship works because Juan notices systems and contradictions while Pedro sees people and takes concrete action. Maria makes both accountable to records, evidence, and the consequences of their choices. A missing town bell reveals forged historical records and an attempt to privatize a common spring. To stop it, they must recover the true history of the town and decide which old customs should be defended—and which should be changed.

## Tone and structural promises

- Age-appropriate Filipino rural ensemble comedy with consequential mystery, genuine growth, and recurring jokes grounded in personality. No cruel trickster ending or endless independent prank sketches.
- **Every episode changes the shared situation**. Discoveries, injuries, relationships, suspects, evidence, and the clock advance. No reset.
- Juan is never merely a clown: his instinct for looking for the simpler mechanism is important, but his avoidance hurts other people and he must change. Pedro is capable and honest, not stupid; his trust and anger can lead him to premature conclusions. Maria is a principal investigator, not a passive prize or love interest.
- Avoid equating being poor or having little formal education with stupidity. Work is valued, but so are attention, cleverness, rest and questioning unfair systems.
- Stories can resolve a specific incident each episode while carrying forward the larger town-wide problem.

## Series artifacts and world rules

1. **Pitong Tunog:** seven bell strikes call a formally valid community meeting under a **fictional Maligaya local covenant**. Not a statement about actual Philippine municipal law.
2. **Old civic bell:** bronze, worn near a spring emblem, damaged in E01, temporarily tested on ground mount. Future repair may need new community vote and revised rules.
3. **Two springs:** one serves the market/town; a second named **Balete** was omitted from newer maps. Existence is supported by an old fragment, not yet independently confirmed by the trio by E01's end.
4. **Red half-leaf seal:** appears on torn map and a message inside bell crate; connects records and physical theft, but precise ownership unproven in E01.
5. **Two-wheeled cart tracks:** initial appearance frames Pedro; later reveal tracks partly planted and concealed with fresh grass.
6. **Leaf-engraved key:** in mystery figure's metal box by E01 ending. Its lock is not revealed yet; do not randomly change its purpose.
7. **Esteban Trading:** grain business and interested buyer of spring rights; the *audience* sees Don Esteban discussing suppression, but Juan only hears the conversation and glimpses the hat/shoes. Investigators don't yet possess a decisive public proof.
8. **Bayan record book:** the original agreement and map pages live at Aling Sela's library. Keep a consistent chain of custody for torn fragments and evidence.

## Episode 1 continuity beat sheet

1. **Prologue, ~0–3 min:** maligned lazy Juan under guava tree; hardworking Pedro brings everyday life; Nena announces missing bell.
2. **Crisis, ~3–6 min:** town rally, Pedro's cart suspected, Kapitana insists on evidence; Maria spots replaced rope.
3. **Stakes, ~6–9 min:** bell's seven strikes protect a public meeting and town spring; Aling Sela reveals expiring private takeover bid by Esteban Trading and torn map.
4. **Investigation, ~9–13 min:** Mang Tino recognizes shipping rope; wagon tracks concealed under grass; Juan discovers river route beneath bridge.
5. **Discovery, ~13–16 min:** bell found in crate, red note, threatened by rising water; Esteban overheard speaking to unknown assistant; Juan records instead of confronting without proof.
6. **Action, ~16–19 min:** Pedro and workshop team prevent bell from sliding into river; Juan chooses real effort; partial map points to Balete spring.
7. **Payoff, ~19–22 min:** workshop safely produces seven strikes, protects deliberation; Kapitana suspends private transfer until scrutiny.
8. **Continuing story, ~22–24 min:** team elects to follow map next morning; mystery key and further warning; the narrative ends on genuine forward movement, not a reset.
